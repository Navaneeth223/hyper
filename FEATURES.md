# Features

## Current Features

### Scenario Generator
Users can enter any hypothetical question.

Example:
"What if humans lived underwater?"

---

### Reality Statistics

Generates:

- Chaos Level
- Fun Level
- Danger Level

---

### Timeline Simulation

Creates:

- Day 1 events
- Week 1 events
- Month 1 events
- Year 1 events

---

### News Generator

Creates fictional breaking news headlines.

---

### Responsive Design

Works on:

- Desktop
- Tablet
- Mobile
